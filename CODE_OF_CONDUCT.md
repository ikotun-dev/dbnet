# Code of conduct

DBNet should be a place where people can do careful technical work without being belittled or harassed.

Be direct about code and generous with people. Give actionable feedback, assume honest mistakes, credit other contributors, and leave room for different levels of experience. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently exclude participants when behavior harms the project or its contributors. Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, and whether a person is willing to correct the behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact options on GitHub. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
